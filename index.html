About Me

Design that started as a favour, and became a craft

I picked up Photoshop and Illustrator through a relative years before I treated it as a career — church posters, small edits, favours for friends. That early groundwork turned into a real practice: today I'm a third-year BCA student building a freelance path around graphic design and video editing, with 3D modeling and AI-assisted web development growing alongside it as newer specialties. Every project, big or small, gets the same attention to detail.
