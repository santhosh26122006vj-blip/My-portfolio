How I Build

AI-accelerated, not AI-automated

Faster Prototyping

AI tools help me move from idea to a working first draft quickly, so more time goes into refining the experience instead of boilerplate setup.

Faster Problem Solving

When something breaks or a tricky feature needs a workaround, AI helps me explore solutions faster — I still test, debug and decide what actually ships.

Backend & Data Workflows

Setting up databases, APIs and server logic goes faster with AI assistance, letting me focus on structuring the data and logic correctly for the project.

Rapid Iteration

Feedback rounds move quicker — I can test layout or copy variations fast, then apply my own design judgement to pick what actually works.

AI is part of my toolkit the same way Photoshop or Premiere Pro is — it speeds up the mechanical parts of the process. The design decisions, the customization, the user experience and the final result are mine: every site here was planned, built and tested by me, one file at a time.
